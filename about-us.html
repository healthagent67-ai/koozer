Our story

A family that has made painting its trade.

Koozer Painting was founded in 1996 by Kevin Koozer, and today he runs the company alongside his son, Nick. As a third-generation painting family, we treat the work with the patience, pride and respect for craft that only comes from growing up in the trade.

Together, Kevin and Nick bring 50 years of combined premier painting experience in downtown Charleston, a city where historic buildings demand a careful hand. That experience is why preservation organizations, architects and restoration firms have trusted us with some of Charleston’s most significant buildings.

Whether it’s a single bedroom, a downtown storefront or a historic landmark, every project gets the same approach. We prepare it properly, use the highest quality materials, and we don’t leave until the job is done and you’re happy.
